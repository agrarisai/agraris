Agraris Litepaper

The registry for AI agents on Robinhood Chain

1. Overview

Agraris is an open registry of AI agents built for Robinhood Chain. It gives builders a place to publish their agents and gives everyone else a place to find, compare, and track them.

2. The problem

Agents on a new chain are scattered across repositories, social posts, and group chats. There is no neutral place to see what exists, what is maintained, and what is worth a closer look.

3. What Agraris does

  • Open publishing: any builder can list an agent with no login and no wallet connection.
  • Discovery: search, category pages, sorting, and a command palette.
  • Comparison: side-by-side comparison of up to three agents with shareable links.
  • Signals: GitHub stars and last-update time, refreshed daily from public repositories.
  • Verified badge: set manually by the Agraris team after review. Verified is not a guarantee of quality, safety, or performance.
  • Distribution: embeddable agent cards for builders' own sites, badge markdown for READMEs, a public read-only API, and an RSS feed.

4. Principles

  • Open: listing is free and permissionless.
  • Neutral: Agraris does not endorse or audit listed agents.
  • Transparent: data comes from public sources, and changes are recorded in the public changelog.
  • Minimal: no accounts, no tracking of individual users.

5. The $AGRARIS token

  • Network: Robinhood Chain (chain ID 4663)
  • Contract: 0x467b7a85f55bd716cb16d59f63b8ccac08852a6c
  • Launched on Pons Family and graduated to Uniswap V4.
  • Current utility (stage one): holders of at least 5,000,000 $AGRARIS (0.5% of supply) can request priority review of an agent listing. Priority review changes the order of review, not the outcome. Every agent is still checked by hand, and Verified is never for sale. Your balance is checked at the time of review. No timeline is promised, and holding the token does not guarantee a badge. This program may change or stop at any time. The registry works the same whether or not you hold the token.
  • Any further utility will be announced publicly before it is added. No future features are promised.
  • Always verify the contract address on the Token page before interacting with any token.

6. Limitations

Agraris is a directory, not a source of on-chain performance data. Listings do not imply traction, volume, or safety. Information may be incomplete or out of date.
